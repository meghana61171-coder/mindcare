About MindCare

MindCare is a mental wellness tracking platform designed to help users monitor their daily moods and emotional health. By logging emotional states and reflecting on daily experiences, users can understand their patterns and maintain mental balance. The application emphasizes simplicity, accessibility, and visualization for effective self-awareness.

Key Features

  • Daily mood logging
  • Simple note-taking for reflections
  • Visual mood overview with dynamic bars
  • User-friendly interface suitable for beginners
  • Responsive and mobile-friendly design
